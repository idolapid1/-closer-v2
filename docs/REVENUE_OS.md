# Revenue operating-system foundation

CLOSER keeps one commercial opportunity (`Lead`) from inquiry through collected payment. The system uses AI for understanding and recommendations, but business truth comes from tenant repositories, validated scheduling, quotes/jobs, payments, and explicit application actions.

## Sales context

Lead source is normalized as WhatsApp, Instagram, website form, email, manual, or import. `sourceReferenceId` deduplicates an external conversation inside one tenant. Priority and objections are typed, persisted, and editable only while the opportunity is open. The commercial stage still comes from `CommercialJourneyService`; no second CRM pipeline or duplicate state machine was introduced.

## Follow-up and reactivation

Each business configures cadence arrays by scenario. Follow-ups preserve sequence step, channel, purpose, attempts, last response, next attempt, owner, draft, result, manual override, and stop reason. Attempt operation keys and scenario idempotency prevent duplicate jobs/messages.

Reactivation candidates are currently limited to old lost opportunities with `NO_LONGER_INTERESTED`, `QUOTE_EXPIRED`, or `UNAVAILABLE`. Marketing consent is mandatory. Owner approval reopens the same opportunity and prepares—not sends—one deterministic follow-up using the configured cadence. Won-customer service-cycle reactivation remains out of scope until recurrence policy is modeled.

## Revenue definitions

- **Potential/open value:** validated appointment/quote/job total; not cash.
- **Booked:** accepted work; not delivery or cash.
- **Completed:** delivered work; not necessarily cash.
- **Collected:** validated payment minus refunds.
- **Generated by CLOSER:** collected cash explicitly verified against matching CLOSER business activities.
- **Recovered by CLOSER:** collected cash explicitly verified as recovery activity; refunds reduce it.
- **Protected/reactivated:** supported attribution kinds, but not shown as production metrics without verified evidence and a settled reporting definition.

Revenue attribution is opt-in and evidence based. The service rejects non-collected events, foreign-tenant/customer/conversation activity, future evidence, operation-key reuse, and changes to an already verified attribution. UI interaction, customer claims, quote value, or assistant output cannot establish revenue.

## Owner Copilot boundary

The current deterministic tool contract supports hot leads, unanswered conversations, revenue truth, reactivation candidates, and an owner-approved prepare-reactivation action. Every call requires an active tenant owner. Mutations additionally require explicit approval and remain inside `CloserService`; audit activity records only tool name and result count. A future LLM may propose these requests but will not receive repository or arbitrary mutation access.

## Connector boundary

WhatsApp, Instagram, website form, and email share a normalized inbound event contract. `ingestInboundLeadEvent` verifies the routed tenant, reuses external conversation identity, deduplicates provider event IDs, and preserves provider occurrence time. Deterministic mock connectors support tests. Production connector classes deliberately throw until backend webhook verification, secrets, signatures, retry storage, and channel credentials exist.

## Current limitations

There is no backend authorization, durable queue, production webhook, live messaging, payment gateway, accounting reconciliation, real AI provider, automatic attribution, background follow-up sender, or recurrence policy. These boundaries are modeled locally so later integrations can be added without bypassing tenant, consent, financial, scheduling, or idempotency rules.
